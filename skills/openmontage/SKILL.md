# OpenMontage-style video work

Treat video work as a pipeline: brief and audience, script/storyboard, asset inventory, generation/editing steps, captions/voice, render settings, and review. Prefer existing local assets and deterministic tools such as FFmpeg when they meet the requirement. Check `ffmpeg`, `yt-dlp`, and any provider-specific tool before promising a render.

External image/video/voice APIs, quotas, and uploads are opt-in. Ask before incurring paid usage or publishing. This is a lightweight integration of the workflow ideas from OpenMontage; its AGPL application and provider-specific runtime are not copied into the coding shim.
