# Marketing skills

On a marketing request, establish the product, audience, positioning, objective, channel, evidence, constraints, and success metric before writing copy. Choose the narrowest workflow: product positioning, copywriting/editing, SEO/content, CRO/experiments, analytics/attribution, launch/GTM, email/cold outreach, retention, or competitive research.

Separate facts from hypotheses. Recommend an experiment and measurable success criterion when the request is optimization rather than a one-off draft. Preserve the user's voice and ask before inventing claims, testimonials, benchmarks, or customer data.

These are adapted routing principles from `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`; the shim keeps the bundle lightweight and does not inject marketing context into unrelated coding sessions.
